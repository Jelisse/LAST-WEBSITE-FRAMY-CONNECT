# Customer order and payment references

Customer format: `FC-OR-26-5827-4191` (18 characters), with `OR` for orders and `PY` for payments. Codes remain stable across languages. The year comes from the record creation date, not the date of a retry. Seven random digits plus one Luhn check digit make the final eight digits; Luhn covers the numeric type code (01/02), year and random digits. It catches all single-digit substitutions and most adjacent digit swaps; it does not prove ownership.

Migration 0038 adds a unique, immutable public_reference to PaySuite product orders and payment records, including historical records. Database allocation runs inside the original transaction, examines up to 16 random candidates, skips existing values and aborts atomically if exhausted. No sequence or sales volume is exposed. The original IDs, provider references, reconciliation, stock reservations, financial records and receipt payloads remain unchanged. Creation still makes at most one provider POST per idempotency key. Reference display lookup failures never suppress provider creation or authorize a new charge.

Tracking accepts OR, PY and legacy 32-character references. Case, whitespace and hyphens are normalized. The same owner/session/email-proof guards protect private details; a public reference is not an access token. Staff exact-reference search covers historical PaySuite purchases, preserving manager authorization. Checkout, payment status/history, fulfilment, new guest emails and pending receipt delivery display branded references. Already-sent emails keep working. Account/payment configuration URLs keep their internal IDs.

Scope: PaySuite product orders and payments. Subscription-request, quote and support-ticket reference families are reserved for later adoption. Official fiscal invoice numbering and external provider numbers are not changed. Legacy sandbox/manual-order identifiers are outside this migration.

Deployment requires a private backup, guarded migration plan/apply, schema/financial integrity checks and fresh Worker build. Tests use local SQLite and simulated providers; no real purchases or emails are generated by tests.
