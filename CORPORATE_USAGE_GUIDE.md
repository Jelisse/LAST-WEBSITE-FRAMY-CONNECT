# Framy Connect corporate team guide

The workspace separates results from administration:

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Reports | Daily trends, team rankings, period/campaign/team filters and aggregate CSV export. |
| Team | Invitations, email authorization, team names, roles, pending requests and access management. |
| Campaigns | Create/close events, assign employees, generate work links/QRs and open campaign reports. |
| Contacts | View voluntarily submitted details and export the displayed, filtered records. |
| How it works | Workflow, permissions, metric definitions and business examples. |
| Company | Company details and Framy approval; contracts and payments remain separate. |

## Practical workflow

1. Submit company details. Framy management approves the company and seat limit. Approval opens Team management; it does not confirm a payment.
2. Choose Invitation, where employees accept and set up their access, or Authorization, where employees consent by email before the leader creates access. Existing accounts retain their credentials and use Invitation.
3. Assign team names and roles. Owners may delegate leaders. Delegated leaders manage ordinary employees and campaigns; employees only see their own results and contacts.
4. Create one campaign per event or initiative. Assign employees and give each their campaign link or QR.
5. Use those work links in the field. Personal links and previously programmed NFC URLs are not automatically attributed to an employer or rewritten. Physical encoding remains a separate authorized workflow.
6. Review Reports using equivalent dates and the same metric. Use Contacts for follow-up only for the purpose the person authorized.

## What the numbers mean

- **Opens** are recorded campaign-attributed profile-opening actions.
- **Clicks** are recorded and validated campaign-attributed actions.
- **Received contacts** are consented form submissions. Request retries are deduplicated; separate submissions are not necessarily unique people.
- **Daily trends** use Maputo time. Today is incomplete; missing days are zero-filled.
- **Team rankings** default to received contacts. Switch to clicks or opens, then compare totals or averages per contributor with at least one recorded action/contact in the selected period.
- Team names reflect current membership groups, not historical team snapshots. Moving a worker can reassign their historical records to their current team in reports.
- A chart does not prove followers, sales, successful contact saving or automatic employee productivity. Privacy exclusions and unavailable analytics can reduce interaction counts.
- Aggregate reports include records beyond the latest contact-detail list. Contact CSV exports displayed rows; report CSV exports aggregate team counts. Bounded reports are labelled as incomplete.

## Uses by business type

| Business | Functions to explore | Useful comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales and field promotion | Teams by area/event, employee campaign QR links, consented enquiries. | Contacts and recorded actions by campaign/team; totals and per-contributor averages. |
| Magazines and media | Journalism, advertising and event teams, professional profiles, reader/partner enquiries. | Campaign interest and contacts received by each department. |
| Real estate | Campaigns per property, fair or area, consultant assignments and work links. | Interested contacts by campaign/team; not confirmed sales. |
| Beauty and wellness | Professional/service profiles, salon/launch campaigns, external booking links. | Recorded interest and enquiries; bookings are handled externally. |
| Technology and consulting | Demo/conference campaigns, service links and information requests. | Team/campaign contacts and activity. |
| Hospitality, restaurants and tourism | Representatives by unit/event, service profiles and consented requests. | Interest by campaign/team; no automatic reservations. |
| Training and associations | Teams by project/fair, information links and expressions of interest. | Campaign activity and contacts without identifying anonymous visitors. |

Contacts remain available for 90 days. Export only when needed and use them for the authorized purpose. Company membership and personal profile access remain separate. The module does not automatically contact leads, manage appointments, confirm sales or debit subscriptions. Actual email delivery and employee acceptance still depend on their existing services and actions.
