# Framy Connect delivery phases

Reference: user-supplied five-phase roadmap, 21 October 2026. HiHello is the structural reference; Framy retains its own brand, capabilities and commercial terms.

| Phase | Deliverable | Completion measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Proposition, public navigation, homepage and company landing page | Visitors understand the offer and choose the correct journey |
| 2 | Guided onboarding, shared application shell and profile editor | Customers publish without assistance and recover from errors |
| 3 | Company templates, employee activation and contact follow-up | A company can launch a consistent team and manage enquiries |
| 4 | Signatures, backgrounds, directory and one CRM connector | Tools work reliably in a pilot company |
| 5 | SSO, provisioning, scanning, wallets and larger deployments | Validated customer demand, security review and load testing |

Phases 1, 2 and 3 are deployed. Phase 2 builds on the existing authenticated customer workspace, versioned draft/public profile API and live preview. Its four steps are identity, contacts, optional personalisation, and review/publication. Phase 3 adds a shared company template, employee card activation/publication and enquiry follow-up with private notes, dates and filtered CSV export. Account creation and login retain existing authentication; company onboarding, payments and paid entitlements retain their server controls. See ACCOUNT_HANDOFF.md for actual verification and release details. Later phases are scope, not promises of available features.

Phase3 refinement deployed21 October2026: company launch dashboard; three Framy card designs with branding/media/field controls; prepared employee drafts and explicit activation; sharing/QR tools; contact drawer with tags, assignment, dates, history and CSV; privacy-aware aggregate card activity. One shared company template. See ACCOUNT_HANDOFF.md for release and verification limits.

Phase4 implementation: email signatures (copy/HTML, essential/QR), downloadable 1080p virtual backgrounds, authenticated published-card company directory and a reviewed HubSpot pilot connector. Shared brand settings and scoped encrypted credentials preserve existing access/privacy controls. See COMPANY_TOOLS.md. Real-company HubSpot connection, mailbox/meeting-app checks and pilot acceptance remain outstanding; the roadmap completion measure is not yet claimed.
