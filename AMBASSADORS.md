# Ambassador programme - 18 October 2026

Customers and existing NFC encoding agents can enrol at `/embaixadores` or from their dashboard. Ambassador membership is separate from the staff role; enrolling never replaces agent permissions. Enrolment generates a unique uppercase first-name prefix plus four random digits, including leading zeroes. Accents are normalized and Chinese name characters remain supported. Existing accounts enrol explicitly; ordinary account registration does not silently enrol every customer.

The default commission is 10%. A manager can change each ambassador's rate between 10% and 15% or deactivate their code. The customer receives 10% off original physical hardware only. Design, delivery and subscription prices are excluded from both discount and commission. For hardware priced at 1,000 MT, checkout hardware is 900 MT; commission is 100 MT at 10% or 150 MT at 15%. Without a code, the original price applies. Self-referrals are rejected.

Prices and eligibility are recalculated on the server. The order snapshots the code, original hardware price, discount, commission rate and commission amount in cents. A database guard rolls back the entire checkout if active ambassador conditions change during creation. Accepted codes survive the existing 30-minute checkout/login draft and are revalidated after restoration. Changing checkout data invalidates previous price acceptance. Provider retries reuse the established payment reference.

The private dashboard shows pending commission, confirmed commission, recorded payouts, available balance and attributed sales. Ambassadors see only their own ledger; managers see all. Tables explicitly limit recent rows to 500 while balances include full history. No buyer contact data is exposed to ambassadors.

Pending checkout does not earn commission. Existing validated payment confirmation moves the referral sale to earned exactly once. Failed payments void pending commissions. Signed refund evidence places earned commission on hold; a manager must document whether to void or restore it after review. Payout recording requires manager access, a unique reference, completed-payment confirmation, a valid date and sufficient confirmed balance. Records are immutable. This records an external transfer already completed; it neither sends money nor provides automatic withdrawals. Refunds after payout can make the balance negative and require manager reconciliation. This ledger is separate from the existing operational finance expense ledger; payouts are not automatically posted there.
